## ➕ My favourite math formulas

Here are some of my favourite math formulas:

$$
y = \alpha x^{2} + \beta x + \gamma
$$

This is the quadratic (parabolic) function: $\alpha$, $\beta$, and $\gamma$ are constant coefficients, $x$ is the independent input variable, and $y$ is the output value.

$$
f(x) = \frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{(x - \mu)^{2}}{2\sigma^{2}}}
$$

This is the normal (Gaussian) probability density function: $f(x)$ denotes the density at point $x$, $\mu$ is the mean, $\sigma$ is the standard deviation ($\sigma > 0$), $\pi$ is the mathematical constant pi, and $e$ is Euler's number.

$$
x_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^{2} - 4ac}}{2a}
$$

This is the quadratic formula for finding the roots $x_{1}$ and $x_{2}$ of a quadratic equation: $a$, $b$, and $c$ are the equation coefficients with $a \neq 0$.

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n} k = \frac{n(n + 1)}{2}
$$

This formula computes the sum of the first $n$ positive integers: $k$ is the summation index running from $1$ to upper bound $n$.

$$
\int_{0}^{\theta} \sin(\omega t + \varphi)\,dt = \frac{\cos(\varphi) - \cos(\omega\theta + \varphi)}{\omega}
$$

This definite integral calculates the accumulated value of a sinusoidal wave over the interval $[0, \theta]$: $t$ is the integration variable, $\omega$ is the angular frequency ($\omega \neq 0$), $\varphi$ is the initial phase shift, and $\theta$ is the upper integration limit.

$$
\begin{cases}
\lambda x + 2y - z = \delta_{1} \\\\
3x - \varepsilon y + 4z = \delta_{2} \\\\
-x + y + 5z = \delta_{3}
\end{cases}
$$

This is a system of three linear equations: $x$, $y$, and $z$ are the unknown variables, $\lambda$ and $\varepsilon$ are scalar parameters, and $\delta_{1}$, $\delta_{2}$, $\delta_{3}$ represent the right-hand side constants.

$$
I_{N} = \begin{bmatrix}
1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\\\
0 & 1 & \cdots & 0 \\\\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\\\
0 & 0 & \cdots & 1
\end{bmatrix}
$$

This is the identity matrix $I_{N}$ of size $N \times N$, where all main diagonal entries equal $1$ and all off-diagonal entries equal $0$.
